# CitySmash

A giant cyber ape climbs and smashes his way across seven tabletop cities, day into night. A game by [Chris Cooley](https://coded.design/), built on [Fizlet](https://fizlet.com/). Play at <https://citysmash.domvirus.com>.

## The game

- **Climb and smash.** Jump at a building's side wall: the jump grabs it. He climbs the side wall, set back behind the front corner, so the windows in reach stay in view. Hanging there, your inside arm grips and climbs; your outside arm smashes the window you tap or click, if it is in reach: three windows in on your floor (close, mid, far: a jab, a hook, a full swing), two in on the floors above and below; only the building you hold, never past the third window in, so the far side means climbing its other edge. Out of reach, he swings at air and loses a moment. Three hits a window: a chip, a crack, a hole; the bottom floor too, from the street (tap a ground-floor window in front of him) or from the wall's lowest hang (the down cells; down held there a moment more lets go). Enough holes and the building shakes, then pancakes floor by floor. Live power boxes, neon signs and roof antennas shock you off the wall. People at the windows are snacks. He grabs with the hand on the side he faces, never behind him or under his own body: on the street a fan of 15 spaces in front of that hand, from the street to over his head (and in by his head, where his body is narrower; never a spot his body covers on screen) (tap something behind him and he turns round first, then grabs); on a wall, one rule from the free hand: if he can punch a window he can grab what's in it (the 7 punch windows), plus the windows two floors up and down from them and the gap's air over his head and under his feet (13). Tap one to eat it, health back: it lights teal at once, his arm reaches to that spot, NOM! and +100 pop up. Soldiers, parachutes and food too; a helicopter by his hand gets swatted (SWAT! +250). Hover (or tap, on a phone) to see the grab reach, outlined where it isn't behind him. The tapped target lights until the action lands: green, a punch; teal, a grab; out of reach it flashes red with TOO FAR, an arrow from him towards it and MOVE CLOSER (CLIMB UP, CLIMB DOWN on a wall). Quick, accurate taps chain a combo (×1 → ×1.25 → ×1.5 → ×1.6, shown by the score): the wind-up and the recovery run faster; a whiff or a 0.6 s pause drops it (the AI rivals only reach ×1.25). **Fight them (v12).** Tap a rival monster (or another player's) in reach, measured from the punching shoulder, and he throws the power punch: the far punch's haymaker on a longer fixed wind-up (0.36 s, `POWER_HIT`), the body coming in at the hit; BOOM!, 16 health, a knockback, and KNOCKED OFF! if it hangs on a wall or stands on a roof (it falls to the street); behind him on the street he turns first; out of reach TOO FAR. Jump down onto one and it is a pounce: 10 health, stunned 1.4 s (stars, no moves); a power punch on a stunned one is a combo (+12). The AI rivals fight back with the same moves at an AI's rate: a power punch at most every 3.2 s, a pounce after a 0.4 s crouch (both fixed: step or jump clear). A small health bar shows over a monster in a fight. The street's enemies are tap targets too (owner: "I can't kill the tanks": a tap on a tank was TOO FAR): a tank, a soldier or a truck anywhere in a band along the ground in front of his punching shoulder (`GROUND_R`, at his feet too; behind him he turns) gets a downward smash; a tank takes 2 (3 from the fourth city, a pip each over it), then KRUNCH!; jump on it and it is crushed. On one screen a hit holds for a few frames (hit-stop); online never. Soldiers, snipers, helicopters and tanks fight back; out of health, a monster shrinks to a tiny figure and runs off the table.
- **Seven cities** in a run, each with its own skyline, a landmark and a feature: Port Lumen (the harbour: a gunboat), Coilworth (the rail yard: a freight train), Brightfield (the stadium: live floodlights, a crowd), Voltmoor (the power plant: surges), Twin Span (the bridge: trucks), Glowgate (the neon strip) and Skyreach (the skyport: two helicopters). Day, then dusk, then night; the score carries over; a city ends when it is flat; the army grows city by city.
- **Garage.** Your monster on a live turntable. The ape: fur, pattern, face, hair (a mohawk, spikes or a crest, in a neon colour), gear (a cyber arm on the smashing arm, a visor, a chest plate, tail spikes and more) and a neon trim. A rival you have beaten: its colour and trim. Every city you flatten unlocks parts. One profile, saved on this device (version 3; older saves and `CS1-…` codes load with only the ape playable), plus a save code to move it.
- **Practice.** PRACTICE on the start menu (or `?mode=practice`): nothing hurts, no army, buildings come back, and a card ticks off each move (seven dots for the seven punches; v12: a sparring dummy, a harmless training rival that never fights back or runs out, to power punch and pounce on). It never touches your profile.
- **Rivals and friends.** SHARDCLAW (a crystal crab-beast), GLOOPMAW (a tentacle blob) and VOLTWING (a storm moth) wreck the same cities. **Beat them to play them:** knock a rival out in a city, playing alone, and it is yours: pick it in the garage (the ape is always yours), in its own colours or a fur colour and neon trim you own; play it and the ape joins the computer rivals. CPU RIVALS: ON/OFF on the start menu (the monsters nobody picked join as AI). One person a device: to play together, PLAY WITH FRIENDS (online: the host opens a room and sends the link; the host is the ape, friends take the rivals). On one screen, extra players come only with gamepads: each connected pad shows a slot on the start menu (press any button to join, pick its monster: all four are open there). A shared score and one each; you can still punch each other off the walls.

Phone: the stick moves and climbs, JUMP jumps, tap to smash and grab. Computer, keys only or keys and the mouse (both always work): WASD or the arrows walk and climb · Space jumps (at a wall: grabs it; on a wall: leaps to the next building across the gap; twice quickly: lets go and drops to the street) · J punches the aimed window (on a wall an aim sits on one of the 7 reach cells, the nearest unbroken window first; Q / E or Tab / Shift+Tab move it, a click moves it too; W or S held: up or down as before; on the street J punches what is in front) · K (or L) grabs (whoever is at the aimed window, else the nearest in front) · click to smash and grab · M mutes · Escape for the menu. Gamepads (players two to four): A jump · X punch · B grab · LB/RB move the aim. The arrows walk and climb too. The reach and grab tables are one const each (`src/round.ts` `REACH`, `GRAB_WALL` (the 7 reach cells first, then the rest from the free hand), `GRAB` (from the facing hand, mirrored with where he faces: `GRAB_X`, `GRAB_Y`), `GRAB_HELI`; the combo ladder `COMBO`); the landing page draws its diagram from them.

## Use it

```js
import { createCitySmash } from "./dist/citysmash.mjs";
const game = createCitySmash({ host: document.getElementById("game"), rooms: "wss://…" });
```

`dist/citysmash.js` is the same as one script (global `citysmash`). No runtime dependencies: the Fizlet bricks it uses are bundled. `game.destroy()` gives the page back exactly as it was.

Online play talks to a fizlet-rooms server (`rooms`, or `?rooms=` on the page; the site's default is in `site/src/site.ts`, `PUBLIC_ROOMS_URL` overrides it at build). The server must allow this site's origin, `https://citysmash.domvirus.com`.

## Develop

```sh
npm ci
npm run check          # typecheck, build dist/, size budget
npm test               # headless: the rules, every city, progression, determinism, the profile, rollback sync, zero allocation
npm run test:settle    # it sleeps when nothing moves
npm run test:browser   # start, the garage, click and tap to smash (in reach, out of reach), eat, a building falls, city 2, 2P, the phone pad, full height at 390; the wall pose per reach cell and the reach kept clear of the monster; the rigs' depth order (turntable, street, wall); depth against the city (no neighbour or side wall over the ape on a wall, every city, both edges, the camera off to each side; the street's points behind or in front by where they are); the grab from the facing hand (in front, behind he turns, never his own body, TOO FAR with its hint, on a wall nothing behind him); practice (its sparring dummy too); the fight (the power punch, TOO FAR, the pounce and the combo, knocked off a wall, the tank at 1 to 3 body widths, the wall's grab outline); online rooms (no hit-stop)
npm run site:build && npm run test:site
```

Source: `src/block.ts` (the destructible buildings), `src/climber.ts` (the wall climber), `src/round.ts` (the run, headless: monsters, army, features, cities), `src/cities.ts` (the seven cities), `src/monster.ts` (the rigs), `src/fx.ts` (the particle kit), `src/paint.ts` (the people, the soldiers, the cracks and holes), `src/scene.ts` (a city on screen), `src/profile.ts` (the save), `src/net.ts` and `src/rollback.ts` (online), `src/game.ts` (title, garage, rooms).
